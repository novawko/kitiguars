kitiguars
intro

The Kitiguars live on their own islands on Earth. The islands have a connecting "bridge" where they travel to the other regions and tribes. However, the Kitiguars do get into war between regions and with tribes.

During every full moon, they gather somewhere on the outskirts of Cavern Region where they discuss any disagreements and recent wars if any. The Kitiguars are the symbol of love and affection so they are trying their best to help everyone in peace and justice. Tribes don't attend the gatherings since they're considered outsiders and are smaller versions of the regions.

Together, we will stay strong.

designing

When creating your Kitiguar, it must follow the below guidelines. Kitiguars that do not fit the species design guidelines do not count as official Kitiguars. They may be deleted from the individual freely without asking for this reason. If you are unsure about something, don't hesitate to ask! I will gladly help.

design rules
  • Kitiguars must have the feral form as the primary image. Any anthro or human forms created for a Kitiguar must remain separated from the main image.
  • Kitiguars may have symbols/tattoos but not cannot have religious nor offensive context regardless if it was not intended in appearance.
  • They cannot have fringes/bangs/hair. They can only have a tuff of fur on the top of their heads at most.
  • Pupils can be slit, round/wide, or blank. Do not shape them as stars, flowers, hearts, etc.
  • All Kitiguars have red blood.
  • Kitiguars may have visible aura, sparkles, and elements floating around them.
  • You may add mutations to your Kitiguar. This includes extra body parts or deformed parts as well. For example, a flat face like Persian cats.
  • All Kitiguars must only have all four paws.
  • All Kitiguars must only have razor sharp teeth.
  • All Kitiguars must have a longish neck, a noodle body, a rabbit-like nose, and long claws that always stay out.
  • They cannot have halos or wings. Wings can be shaped aura magic, however.
anatomy

Kitiguars have a very similar anatomical structure to that of cats. They have very flexible spines, starting from their neck/skull all the way down to the tip of their tail. Also, a Kitiguar's claws cannot unsheathe.

Just like their skeletons, Kitiguars have a lot of similarities to cats. All of their organs work just the same as cats. They usually eat meat but also herbs, so they're omnivores. The claws are built and structured stronger than cats as so they don’t break off easily.

Kitiguars only have paws, no hooves or other types of feet. The teeth are very similar to rats but some can come as sabertooth or with an underbite... or both. Also, Kitiguars are commonly seen with two different types of tongues, pointed and forked. Their tongues are long like snakes.

pelt

Kitiguars have the same patterns and pelt as cats. They come in various types of pelt colors and patterns. They're normally natural-colored but it's more rarer for a bright and neon Kitiguar to be born. Kitiguars also can dye their pelt to look like they were born unnatural. This is uncommon for a Kitiguar to do.

height

Adult Kitiguars are as big as a Siberian Husky dog (roughly 24 inches tall) or as small as an average cat (about 12 inches tall). Baby Kitiguars are as small as a baby kitten (3 to 6 inches long).

Kitiguars can also be undersized or oversized. Usually, an oversized Kitiguar are highly likely to be appointed as their group's leader depending on their role. Otherwise, an undersized Kitiguar are usually ranked lower than their peers.

Male Kitiguars are generally larger than female Kitiguars.

However, there are large Kitiguars that can be as slightly taller than an average grown man (roughly 8 feet tall). These types of Kitiguars are called Alphas.

culture

Kitiguars love to follow human culture and get very curious and fascinated about items made by humans. Otherwise, Kitiguars generally collect rocks, shells, pearls, bones, stones, crystals and such.

locations

Kitiguars are located anywhere throughout the world but are primary located between Asia and Europe. Some Kitiguars have special traits in order to survive the climate at their living space. For example, Kitiguars have long fur in the Arctic to help keep warm. While Kitiguars in the desert have very short fur to being hairless to prevent from overheating.
